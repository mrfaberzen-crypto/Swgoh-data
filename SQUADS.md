# Ranked squads — reviewed 27 October 2026

These are recommendations for Faberzen's saved roster, not global meta rankings or guaranteed counters. The baseline is the saved 21 October TW/GAC plan, checked against the 27 October roster. The main 5v5 list preserves its eleven allocations. BAM and Bastila are additions in the projects section, not replacements.

## Ranking method

The numbered list is a curated order of general usefulness with the current roster: established synergy, character investment, completeness and the user's reported battle experience. Nearby positions are judgment calls. It is not a GP score, simulated win rate, deployment list or farm priority. Check the actual opponent before selecting the lowest-cost safe counter. Live roster refresh updates gear, stars and equipped-mod warnings, but does not automatically re-rank squads or verify speed, abilities, datacrons, defence assignments or available attacks.

SLKR and Grievous are deliberately unranked build projects until their current blockers are resolved. Six equipped mods means full slots, not good mods or battle readiness. Manual notes are dated and must be reviewed after major upgrades.

## Maintenance

Edit `squads.js` to revise coaching order, membership, role or caveats. First member is leader. Set `project: true` for alternatives or builds outside the main allocation. The UI displays live missing-character/mod/gear warnings and explicitly identifies overlaps between projects and main teams. Re-run the squad invariant and dashboard checks when changing lineups.
