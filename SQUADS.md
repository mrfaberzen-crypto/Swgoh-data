# Ranked squads — reviewed 10 November 2026

These are roster-specific coaching judgments, not measured win rates or guaranteed counters. They use the 10 November 2026, 12:35 AEDT snapshot. The main allocation in each format contains no duplicate selected characters; projects may overlap and say so explicitly.

## Ranking method

Order reflects current investment, synergy, completeness and tactical usefulness. Nearby positions are judgment calls. Check the actual enemy, datacron, omicrons, speeds and your locked availability before using the lowest-cost sufficiently safe counter. Public roster data does not establish current defence, used squads or a live opponent.

SLKR is promoted because R7 and finished mods make him operational, but the public export does not identify his two user-reported zetas or evidence the ultimate. Grievous is promoted because his six mods are now level 15 and the whole droid squad is R5; support mods and key abilities still restrict its safe matchups. Neither promotion is a measured win-rate claim.
